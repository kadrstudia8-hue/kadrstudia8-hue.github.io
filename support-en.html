What to include so we can help faster

  • Your account e-mail.
  • Computer: Windows or Mac, graphics card (if you know it), App version ("About and support").
  • What you did and what happened — a screenshot.
  • For payments: date, amount, network, transaction hash (TxID).
  • The error log, if we ask: on Windows the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\KADR Studio, on Mac ~/Library/Application Support/KADR Studio. Do not send providers.json — it holds your keys.

Common problems

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That is how Windows greets new apps without a publisher signature. Click "More info" → "Run anyway". [PLACEHOLDER: remove once the installer is signed]

Mac says the developer cannot be verified

"System Settings" → "Privacy & Security" → "Open Anyway". You do this once.

Account is "waiting for activation"

The account is opened by a licence or a subscription (a ⚡ top-up is only a balance for the cloud). Once you have bought one, write to us from your account e-mail — we activate it during working hours; then press "Check again".

I topped up but no ⚡ arrived

Check that you sent the exact amount in USDT or USDC on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). Wait a few minutes and press "Check now" in the "Energy" window. If nothing arrives within 30 minutes, write to us with the transaction hash. More in transfer mistakes.

The generator downloads slowly or will not install

The generator is about 28 GB; you need a stable connection and disk space. If the connection drops, press the install button again — the download resumes. An NVIDIA card with 8–12 GB of video memory is needed.

The power went off in the middle of a video

Open the App again — the video continues from where it stopped.

How do I delete my account and data?

Local data — "Settings" in the App. The account — write to us from its e-mail. More in the Privacy Policy.
