4a. Free photos without keys (Openverse)

When nothing that draws frames is connected, the app finds photos for the scenes through Openverse (the WordPress Foundation's search engine for openly licensed images; no key, rate-limited). Only licences that allow commercial use and changes are taken: CC0, Public Domain Mark and CC BY. Not taken: NC (non-commercial), ND (no derivatives — a frame in a video is cropped and moved) and SA (share-alike would put the whole video under CC BY-SA). Photos with people are skipped unless the scene is about people: the buyer has no release from them.

Attribution. For every photo the app keeps the author, title, licence and source page and adds a “Photo: author, “title”, licence — link” block to the publishing texts (YouTube description and the “… — публикация.txt” file) by itself. CC BY requires it: keep the line in the description (on TikTok/Reels — in a comment or the profile bio). Openverse does not verify licences: if a photo matters to your business, open the link and check the licence on the page. Made with Openverse; Openverse does not endorse or certify KADR Studio. Not wanted — in the video settings choose “Scene frames: No frame generation”.
