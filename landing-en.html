How it works

Three steps from idea to MP4

No timeline editing. You describe what you want — the program does the rest.

1

Write the idea

One sentence like «Remember the Dendy?» or your own ready text. Pick a format — 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5 — and a length from 15 seconds to 30 minutes. You can paste a whole brief or article — up to 30,000 characters.

2

Press ➤

KADR writes the script (Claude, OpenAI or Gemini with your key), voices it, draws the frames, composes background music and places word-by-word subtitles.

3

Take the finished video

An MP4 with loudness levelled for social media. Want changes? Ask the built-in AI assistant and press «Apply».

Timing on an RTX 3060: a frame takes about a minute, a live scene about 2.5 minutes, a 20-minute film about 4 hours. Without keys or a graphics card a video from your text is ready in a minute or two.

Honest about requirements

What you need for everything to work

Required

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) or macOS 12 Monterey and later — a Mac with Apple M1–M4 or Intel.
  • For free frames, music and live scenes — an NVIDIA card with 8–12 GB of video memory. The program downloads and installs the generator (~28 GB) by itself.
  • A Mac with Apple M1–M4 and 16 GB of memory or more draws frames itself; on other Macs online services make the frames.
  • Without a suitable graphics card you can use paid picture APIs or the KADR cloud.

Good to know in advance

  • Speed depends on the graphics card: on an RTX 3060 a frame takes about a minute, a 20-minute film about 4 hours.
  • Power cut? Nothing is lost: the program continues from the same place.
  • The AI script and ElevenLabs voices work through your own keys — their plans are paid to the services themselves.
  • First launch on a Mac: macOS asks about an unknown developer — «System Settings» → «Privacy & Security» → «Open Anyway». Only once.

FAQ

FAQ

How do I get access to the program?

Download KADR Studio for your computer and sign up in it with your e-mail. Then write to a support manager — they activate your account and every feature opens. It usually takes a few minutes during working hours. [SUPPORT CONTACT AND HOURS]

What are tokens and the balance?

If you have no powerful graphics card, frames, live scenes and music can be made in the KADR cloud: the program rents a graphics card for the video and takes tokens from your balance. You top up the balance right in the program. What a token costs and how many a video takes — [TOKEN PRICES — fill in]. With your own NVIDIA card (or a Mac on an Apple chip with 16 GB+) frames are drawn for free and no tokens are needed.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes: macOS 12 and later, a Mac with an Apple M1–M4 chip or Intel. On a Mac with an Apple chip and 16 GB of memory or more the computer draws the frames itself; on others — the KADR cloud or services with a key. Voice, subtitles, music with a key, editing and assembling work on any Mac. On first launch macOS asks for permission once — «System Settings» → «Privacy & Security» → «Open Anyway».

Do I need paid AI keys?

No. Without keys your own text becomes the script and the voice-over is done by free neural voices (Ukrainian, Russian, English) or the system voices. Claude, OpenAI or Gemini keys let the AI write the script for you; an ElevenLabs key — for ElevenLabs voices, including your cloned ones.

How do I connect a key?

Just paste it into the program — it recognises the service and picks the models.

What graphics card do I need?

For free frames, music and animation — an NVIDIA card with 8–12 GB of video memory; the program installs the generator by itself. Without such a card, frames come from services with a key or free photos from Pexels and Pixabay, and with no keys at all a free voice reads your text over a living colour backdrop.

Where do my data and keys go?

Videos, scripts and projects are stored on your computer. The account is needed only to activate the program and for the token balance; there is no telemetry. Keys are stored encrypted: on Windows by the system (DPAPI), on a Mac in the Keychain. If you connect outside services (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, ElevenLabs), request texts go straight to those services with your key.

Which formats and lengths?

9:16 for Shorts, TikTok and Reels, 16:9 for YouTube, plus 1:1 and 4:5. From 15 seconds to 30 minutes.
