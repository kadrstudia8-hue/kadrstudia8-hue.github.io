How it works

Three steps from idea to MP4

No timeline editing. You describe what you want — the app does the rest.

1

Write an idea

One sentence or your own text — up to 30,000 characters. Format 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5, length from 15 seconds to 30 minutes. Or pick a template: Facts, Top 5, Story, Product ad, Tutorial.

2

Press ➤

KADR writes the script (Claude, OpenAI or Gemini with your key), voices it, draws the frames, animates key scenes, composes music and places word-by-word subtitles. Want to see the frames first — turn on the storyboard.

3

Take the finished video

An MP4 with social-ready loudness. The app checks the video itself and tells you in plain words what to fix; any frame redraws with one button.

Timing on an RTX 3060: about a minute per frame, about 2.5 minutes per live scene, about 4 hours for a 20-minute film. Without keys or a GPU, a video from your text is ready in a minute or two.

Good to know in advance

  • Speed depends on the GPU: on an RTX 3060 a frame takes about a minute, a 20-minute film about 4 hours.
  • A power cut loses nothing: the app resumes where it stopped.
  • AI scripts and ElevenLabs voices use your own keys — you pay those services their own rates. A Gemini key is free.
  • First launch on Mac: macOS warns about an unidentified developer — “System Settings” → “Privacy & Security” → “Open Anyway”. Only once.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it hard?

No. An idea field, a format, a length — and ➤. There are templates (Facts, Top 5, Story, Product ad, Tutorial), 12 picture styles and 12 ready characters. You just paste a key — the app works out which service it belongs to. The frame generator installs inside the app with one button.

Do I need an NVIDIA GPU?

For free frames, music and live scenes — yes, an NVIDIA card with 8–12 GB of VRAM (on an Apple M Mac with 16 GB+ the Mac draws frames itself). Without one, frames come from image services with a key or free Pexels and Pixabay photos, and with no keys at all a free voice reads your text over an animated colour backdrop. KADR Cloud for computers without a GPU is coming soon.

Won't it look like cheap AI mush?

Watch the showreel above — that is what comes out of the app. Before rendering you can turn on the storyboard and redraw any frame. After rendering, “Video check” finds flaws and names them in plain words, and redraws gross defects itself once. With a Gemini key, an AI checks live scenes: a bad one is re-shot, and after two failures the scene stays a good still.

How long does a video take?

It depends on the GPU. On an RTX 3060: about a minute per frame, about 2.5 minutes per live scene, about 4 hours for a 20-minute film. Without keys or a GPU, a video from your text is ready in a minute or two.

How much does it cost?

The trial is free: 3 videos up to 60 seconds. A lifetime licence is $59 for the first 100 buyers, then $99: everything on your PC, 1 year of updates. Instead of the licence — a subscription at $9 a month or $79 a year. Done-for-you setup — $99–149. The cloud (⚡ packs from $10) is coming soon. To buy, write to kadrstudia8@gmail.com; card payment is coming soon. With your own GPU frames cost nothing per image, and AI-service keys (optional) are paid to those services.

How do I get access?

Download KADR Studio for your computer and sign up in it with your e-mail. Without paying, the trial works (3 videos). Buy a licence or a subscription — write to kadrstudia8@gmail.com from your account e-mail and we activate it: the mark and the limits go away. It usually takes a few minutes during working hours. A ⚡ top-up is only a balance for the cloud; it does not open the app. Contacts are on the Support page.

What is Energy ⚡?

⚡ is KADR's internal unit for the cloud: without a strong GPU, frames, live scenes and music are rendered in KADR Cloud on our servers, and ⚡ is charged as frames are ready; you see the price before it starts. ⚡ is not money or a cryptocurrency and can't be transferred. With your own NVIDIA GPU (or an Apple-chip Mac with 16 GB+) frames are free and you don't need ⚡. The cloud and ⚡ prices are coming soon.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes: macOS 12 and later, Macs with Apple M1–M4 or Intel. On Apple-chip Macs with 16 GB+ the computer draws frames itself; on others — image services with a key, and soon KADR Cloud. No live scenes on Mac yet. Voice, subtitles, editing and rendering work on any Mac. On first launch macOS asks once — “System Settings” → “Privacy & Security” → “Open Anyway”.

Do I need paid AI keys?

No. Without keys your own text becomes the script, and free neural voices (English, Ukrainian, Russian) or system voices do the voice-over. Claude, OpenAI or Gemini keys (Gemini's is free) let the AI write the script for you; an ElevenLabs key unlocks ElevenLabs voices, including your cloned ones.

Where do my data and keys go?

Videos, scripts and projects stay on your computer. The account is only for activation and the ⚡ balance; there is no telemetry. Keys are stored encrypted: on Windows by the system (DPAPI), on Mac in the Keychain. If you connect external services (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, ElevenLabs), the request text goes directly to them with your key. More — Privacy Policy.

What formats and lengths?

9:16 for Shorts, TikTok and Reels, 16:9 for YouTube, plus 1:1 and 4:5. From 15 seconds to 30 minutes.
