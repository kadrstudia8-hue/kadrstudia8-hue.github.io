4. Services with your keys

If you connect your own key, the App sends the request text — and for some services frames or audio — directly to that service (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, ElevenLabs, Pexels, Pixabay, etc.), not through us. The free neural voices use Microsoft's online speech service — the narration text is sent there. Those companies process data under their own policies.

With a Google Gemini key: each live scene's still and a small, downscaled copy of its clip are sent to Gemini to check the motion's physics; the whole finished video is sent to Gemini (Google keeps an uploaded file for 48 hours) only for the “AI director review” — when you press its button or if you switched on “Automatic review of a finished video by the AI director” in the settings (off by default).
